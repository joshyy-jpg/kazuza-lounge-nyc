01Our Story

Old roots.
New energy.

Since 2005, Kazuza Lounge has called Avenue A home. Here in the East Village, our nights bring together cocktails, hookah, food, and music — with room to settle in and see where the evening takes you.

Our Mediterranean roots meet the rhythm of New York. Start with a familiar favorite or try one of our signature cocktails. Gather around a pitcher, choose a hookah blend, and make yourself comfortable beneath the warm lights. Whether it’s a catch-up with friends, a date, or a celebration, there’s space to make the night your own.

As the evening unfolds, the energy changes. Live DJs take over Thursday through Saturday from 9PM, carrying the room into the early hours. The kitchen keeps going until 3:30AM, and our doors stay open until 4AM every night.

Old roots keep us connected to the neighborhood. New energy comes from everyone who walks through the door. Find us at 107 Avenue A — come for a drink, settle into the room, and stay a little longer.

02Blends

The coals get
turned all night

Coconut-husk coals, cut and lit to temperature. Fresh fruit heads when you want them, ice pipes when the room runs warm. A session lasts about ninety minutes, and the coals get turned the whole way through — that part isn't left to you.

Coconut Coals

Natural husk, low ash, no chemical bite. Cut square and brought to the table already lit.

Fruit Heads

Hollowed pineapple, melon or grapefruit in place of clay. Slower burn, softer smoke.

Ice Pipes

Chilled stem for the nights the room runs hot. Ask when you order, not after.
